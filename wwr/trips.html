Your Adventure Awaits

Big Kahuna

Take on the ultimate rafting challenge with the Big Kahuna! This action-packed adventure features powerful rapids, steep drops, and heart-pounding waves designed for those seeking an adrenaline-fueled experience on the river.

Little Kahuna

The Little Kahuna is the perfect introduction to white-water rafting! Enjoy smaller rapids, gentle waves, and plenty of excitement while taking in the beautiful surroundings. It's a great choice for families, beginners, and first-time rafters.

Slippery Showdown

Prepare for an unforgettable battle against the river in the Slippery Showdown! Navigate challenging rapids, splash through rushing waves, and work together with your crew to conquer every twist and turn. This adventure is perfect for rafters ready to test their skills.
